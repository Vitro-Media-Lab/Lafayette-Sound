How It Works

Our Installation Process

01

Site Assessment

We visit your space, evaluate acoustics, existing infrastructure, and usage requirements before recommending any equipment.

02

System Design

Our team designs a system tailored to your space — documented with diagrams, equipment lists, and a full project scope before any work begins.

03

Installation

Clean, professional installation with fully concealed cabling, proper mounting, and zero shortcuts. We treat your space like our own.

04

Testing & Tuning

Every system is tuned and tested to perform at its best in your specific room — including acoustic measurement and signal path verification.

05

Training

We train your staff to operate the system confidently. No black boxes, no mystery — your team should own the technology.

06

Ongoing Support

We remain available for service, upgrades, and support. A permanent installation is a long-term relationship.
